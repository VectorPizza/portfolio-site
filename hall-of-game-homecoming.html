Hall of Game: Homecoming 2025

The brand, event assets and broadcast graphics for Full Sail University’s annual esports homecoming, where varsity takes on alumni for a place on the Hall of Game trophy.

My role
Sole designer and creative lead, concept to live event
Scope
Look and feel, logo, animation, digital art
Also delivered
Print, swag, venue install, stream broadcast graphics
When
Sunday, March 9, 2025
Orlando Health Fortress, Full Sail University, and live on Twitch

01The brief

A one-day esports homecoming: Armada varsity teams against returning alumni across Apex Legends, League of Legends and Halo Infinite, with the winners going on the Hall of Game trophy. It opened Hall of Fame week, and it had to land with two audiences at once: the people in the room and everyone watching the stream.

The hard part was scale: one direction carried through every asset, concept to completion, with the energy an esports crowd expects, in the room and on screen.

“Hall of Game is a highly anticipated event for students and alumni alike.”

Sari Kitelyn, Director of Esports, Full Sail University

02The idea

Homecoming tradition and an esports rivalry, with a more modern, energetic look than the year before. Everything was built to feel like a college game day.

03The system

Mark, type, color and graphic language
Primary mark. Drawn from college football so the event reads as game day at a glance. The shield carries over from the year before, redrawn sharper, and splits into team marks, A for Alumni and V for Varsity, for scoreboards, overlays and champion screens.
Helvetica Neue Black CondensedHomecomingYellowtailWelcome Home
Typography. A heavy condensed face for esports energy, and a script for the personal, traditional note: the tagline “Welcome Home” and the word “Team” on scoreboards.
Armada Orange#ED5F22
Dark Gray#222222
Light Gray#F3F3F3
Color. Straight from the Armada palette.
Team lockups. One construction, two colorways, carried through the team desks, venue screens, scoreboards and fan posters.

04Rollout

One idea across every screen and surface
Promotion. Social posts with the matchup, the date and where to watch.
Broadcast and motion. Animated backgrounds, transitions, looping logos, lower thirds, schedule, scoreboards and game overlays.
Walk-in loop
Varsity win
Alumni win
In the venue. One layout system mapped across every screen in the room: wall banners, the minitron, team desks, the main tron and the ribbon ring. A walk-in loop ran all day, and each team had a win scene that took over the room.
Fan posters. A sign for each side of the crowd, plus one for the end of every match.
Swag and print. The pennants were the most popular takeaway of the day. There were also credentials, buttons, stickers and an event T-shirt.

05Owning it end to end

I designed every piece: the brand, the venue screens and the stream.

A call I madeTreating it as collegiate sports, not another esports event. Full Sail has no traditional college game day, so this gave students one, and it set the right tone for current students against returning alumni.
Making it work in the roomEvery screen in the room had its own brightness, resolution and color, so on site I tuned color, content scale and animation speed screen by screen until the whole space read as one system.
