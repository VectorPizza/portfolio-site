Hall of Game: Paragon

The 2023 Hall of Game. That year’s Hall of Fame theme was the supernatural. The name came from the word itself: a paragon is the best of its kind, which is what every team was competing to be.

My role
Design lead
Scope
Key art, logo and animation, poster, arena screens, credentials and pins
Where
Orlando Health Fortress, Full Sail University
Year
2023

01The art

Making the art was the best part of this one. The key art set the look, and everything else came from it: the intro title, the poster and the screens in the arena.
