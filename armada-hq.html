Armada HQ

Full Sail’s first ground-up esports center: 45 gaming stations, a console lounge and a soundproofed broadcast room, designed for the Armada.

My role
Lead designer
Scope
Space design, color palette, wall graphics for every room, jersey hallway mockup
Worked with
Art director, on lighting
Year
2025

01The brief

Since 2018 the Armada had made do with one small room. The new space had to host varsity matches, open play for every student, and community events and workshops.

I created the Armada brand when the program started as a student club, so this was a chance to take it from screens into a real space.

Four rooms, one identity.

06Leading it

I designed the space and chose its colors, then created the wall graphics for every room, working with our art director on the lighting so the graphics and the room read as one.

A call I madeFilling an orange wall with posters from past Armada events, so the new space carried the program’s history.
Design to installPainted walls and vinyl. Every wall was planned against the layout first, so install went in without surprises.
