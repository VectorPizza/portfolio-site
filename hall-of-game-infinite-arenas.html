Hall of Game: Infinite Arenas

The 2022 edition of Hall of Game: an arena-based collegiate esports tournament across three high-intensity team games, with Armada varsity against returning alumni.

My role
Sole designer and creative lead, concept to live event
Scope
Logo, 3D modeling, 2D and 3D animation, digital art, arena screens, social and marketing
Where
Orlando Health Fortress, Full Sail University
Year
2022

01The brief

The same format as every Hall of Game: one day, three games, Armada varsity against returning alumni, with the winners going on the trophy.

02The idea

Hall of Game kicks off Hall of Fame week, and in 2022 the Hall of Fame theme was time. Infinite Arenas came out of that theme, and a tunnel of light became the base for every screen.

03In the arena

Stage, ring and screens on event day

04Promotion

Animated social posts, one per game

05Owning it end to end

I designed all of it, from the logo and its 3D animation to the arena screens and the social campaign.
