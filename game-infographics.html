Game Infographics

A social series for Full Sail Armada: six games, each broken down into the facts that matter to players, and linked to the Full Sail grads who worked on it.

My role
Research, planning and design
For
Full Sail Armada social
Series
Six games, three sizes each
Year
2020–2021

01The brief

The brief was a list of games. I researched each title, decided what to show and designed every one in three sizes. Each borrows its look from its game but keeps the same structure: what the game is, the numbers, and the grads behind it.

02One game, three sizes

Four games, each rebuilt for every format

Same content, three layouts. The panels stack for the tall post and spread into columns for the wide ones.

Room to say more. The widescreen versions carry extra content, like league formats and the Armada varsity rosters with each player’s degree.
