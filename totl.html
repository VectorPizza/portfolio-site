Project overview

Enter a delivery ZIP once, and every price on a store page gets a second line with what you’ll actually pay.

Tax rates ship inside the extension, so there’s no server lookup.
Built for Chrome and Firefox.

The price you see isn’t the price you pay

Most U.S. stores show prices before sales tax. The total only appears at checkout, surprising shoppers with a higher price.

Merchant learning

Totl reads the tax a store charges at checkout and reuses it on the next visit.
  • Reads the store’s taxAt checkout, Totl reads the tax the store charged.
  • Checks itTwo matching carts make it trusted. A mismatch starts over.
  • Uses it next timeThe cart shows the store’s tax before the store does.
